# Cunningham Thinking Time Questions Bank

Organized by domain. Pull the relevant section(s) when running a Thinking Time session. Don't dump the full list — select 3–5 high-leverage questions per session.

---

## The Unasked Question — Meta Questions

These go first, before any domain-specific questions.

- What question, if answered, would give me the most clarity on this situation?
- What am I not asking that I should be asking?
- What would a skeptical board member ask that I haven't asked myself?
- What is the real problem here, as opposed to what I think the problem is?
- If I were fired and a new CEO came in tomorrow, what decision would they make — and why?
- What could I do that would make this problem worse? (Inversion)
- What question am I afraid to ask?

---

## Revenue & Sales

- Why aren't my sales doubling?
- What is the core obstacle preventing the doubling of our revenue? (Strategic — not "why are sales low?")
- Who is my target customer — specifically, not generally?
- Who are the target customers of my competitors, and how do they differ from mine?
- What markets should we target to double sales?
- What does our ideal customer look like, and what do they actually buy us for?
- What is the one thing our best customers have in common?
- Where is revenue leaking — and what's allowing it to leak?
- What would I need to believe about our customers for this revenue problem to make sense?

---

## Strategy & Focus

- What am I optimizing for? (The great question — ask this constantly)
- What is the one major thing that, if accomplished, would have the most significant impact on this business?
- What are the three things I could begin doing that would get me 80% of the way there?
- Where am I confusing activity with progress?
- What is our core business, and are we actually doing it — or have we drifted?
- What do we need to stop doing to make room for what matters?
- What would we do if we could only do three things this quarter?
- What am I tolerating that is costing us growth, money, or time?

---

## Risk & Decisions

- What is the upside?
- What is the downside — specifically, not categorically?
- Can I live with the downside?
- What would have to be true for this to work?
- What's the evidence that it is true?
- What are the top three assumptions embedded in this plan?
- What happens after what happens? (2nd-order consequences)
- What's the worst realistic case — not the catastrophic case, but the realistic worst case?
- If this goes completely wrong, what does my situation look like 12 months from now?
- Am I betting the business on this? Should I be?
- What is the cost of being wrong here?
- What is the cost of not deciding?

---

## People, Culture & Leadership

- What am I tolerating in my team that I know I shouldn't be?
- What would I do if I knew the outcome would be the same regardless of effort — and what does that tell me?
- Where have I abdicated my CEO responsibilities, and what do I need to do about it?
- What are the three non-negotiable standards in this business — and are they actually non-negotiable?
- What belief does an underperforming employee have to hold for this problem to persist?
- What are the clear expectations I've set — and how do I know they're clear?
- Where am I leading by example in a way I'm not proud of?
- Who on my team is a passenger, and what am I doing about it?
- What is the culture I've created by what I tolerate?

---

## Execution & Systems

- What is the machine — the specific sequence of steps — that gets me from Point A to Point B?
- What are the critical drivers: the 3–5 things that, if consistently executed, guarantee the outcome?
- What gets measured? (What gets measured gets done)
- What are the leading indicators — not lagging ones — that tell me if we're on track?
- Where does execution break down in this business, and what's causing it?
- What is the simplest possible system that would work here?
- What are the standards, and are they written down and enforced?
- Where am I hoping for a result without building a system to produce it?

---

## Financial & Capital

- How much money would I have right now if I could unwind my three worst financial decisions?
- What is the financial model underlying this plan — and have I stress-tested it?
- What assumptions does this financial projection rest on?
- Where am I using revenue as a proxy for profit?
- What is the cash flow picture — not the P&L picture?
- What is the break-even, and when do I hit it?
- Where am I confusing a financing decision with a business decision?
- What is this decision actually costing me — in time, capital, and opportunity cost?

---

## Owner vs. Operator Mode

- Am I working in the business or on the business right now?
- What would happen to this business if I took a month off? What does that tell me?
- What am I doing that someone else should be doing?
- What am I not doing that only I can do?
- Where have I optimized for easy instead of outcomes?
- What does an owner do that I'm not doing?
- What problem keeps recurring because I haven't built a system to prevent it?

---

## Lessons Learned / Post-Mortem

- As I look back at my most significant losses and mistakes, what are the most important patterns?
- Where am I making the same mistakes again?
- What rules and disciplines do I need to put in place to avoid repeating this?
- What did I know before this went wrong that I chose to ignore?
- What would I have needed to believe for this to have gone differently?
- What is the lesson here — and what changes tomorrow because of it?
